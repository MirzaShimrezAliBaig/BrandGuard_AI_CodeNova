Cookie & Storage Notice

BrandGuard AI uses only storage necessary for the prototype's functions.

Essential session cookie

When team authentication is enabled, an HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax cookie stores the server-managed session. It is required for sign-in and workspace access.

Local demo storage

The browser demo uses localStorage to retain sample knowledge, review history and settings on the same device. You can clear this data from Settings.

No advertising or analytics cookies

No advertising, tracking or analytics SDK is included in this build.

Consent

The cookie notice shown in the app explains the essential storage used by the prototype. There are no optional marketing cookies to opt into.
