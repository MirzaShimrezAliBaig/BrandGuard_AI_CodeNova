Privacy Policy

BrandGuard AI · CodeNova India 2026 · Non-commercial hackathon prototype.

This prototype is designed to demonstrate pre-publication brand governance. It is not a commercial service and does not intentionally collect data beyond what is needed for the demo or optional team workspace.

Data we may process

  • Content you choose to submit for review.
  • Approved knowledge entered into a workspace.
  • Account email/display name when team authentication is enabled.
  • Review decisions and audit events in a configured workspace.
  • Local browser demo data stored in localStorage.

Data minimisation

Do not submit passwords, payment information, government identifiers, health information, children's data, or other sensitive information into the demo. The application is intended for organisational content, not personal records.

Hosted AI

Hosted AI is opt-in. When enabled, the draft and retrieved approved evidence are sent from the server to the AI provider configured by the workspace operator. API credentials remain server-side and are not placed in frontend code.

Cookies and local storage

The team workspace uses an essential HttpOnly session cookie. The local demo uses browser localStorage for demo state. No advertising or analytics cookies are included.

Retention and deletion

Local demo data can be cleared from Settings. Signed-in users can submit a cloud deletion request from Settings. Shared workspace records are reviewed before deletion so one member cannot accidentally erase a team's audit history.

Children

BrandGuard AI is not directed to children. Do not submit children's personal data.

Contact

For this hackathon prototype, use the project repository/submission contact supplied by the CodeNova team. No separate commercial support channel is represented by this prototype.
